Responsible sourcing conversations

Better choices begin with better questions.

These are general topics buyers may discuss when developing apparel products. They are not sustainability claims, targets, certifications, or verified practices of a real company.

Topics to explore

Make requirements specific and verifiable.

Material and process decisions involve trade-offs. Ask for current, product-specific evidence and define what matters to your business before making claims.

01

Responsible sourcing

Clarify material origin, supplier requirements, documentation, and the scope of any sourcing standard being discussed.

02

Material efficiency

Consider fabric utilization, marker planning, and specification choices that may influence material use.

03

Waste reduction

Ask how cutting waste, packaging, and production remnants are measured, managed, and documented.

04

Water and energy

For relevant processes, request site- and process-specific data rather than relying on broad statements.

05

Supply-chain transparency

Define which production sites, processes, and upstream suppliers need to be disclosed and reviewed.

06

Product durability

Discuss construction, care instructions, testing criteria, and expected product use with the development team.

A practical starting point

Set goals that can be reviewed.

Any sustainability goal should have a defined scope, baseline, owner, measurement method, and review date. This concept does not set or report company targets.

  1. 01

    Define the product scope

    Identify the product, production stages, sites, and materials under review.

  2. 02

    Request supporting evidence

    Ask for current documentation relevant to the exact claim and supply-chain scope.

  3. 03

    Agree how progress is measured

    Set a baseline and use a consistent method before comparing outcomes.

  4. 04

    Review and communicate carefully

    Document limitations and avoid extending a result beyond what the evidence supports.
